Delete your Paycheck Pilot account

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Paycheck Pilot and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account & Privacy.
  3. Tap Delete My Account, type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Request deletion without the app

Email support@paycheckpilot.app from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We'll delete it and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your login and account credentials
  • Your financial data — accounts, bills, income, transactions and goals
  • Your push-notification subscriptions
  • Your synced data on our servers

If you're in a shared household, only your own membership and personal data are deleted; the household's shared data stays for the other members. Deletion can't be undone — export a backup first from Settings → Data Management if you want a copy.
