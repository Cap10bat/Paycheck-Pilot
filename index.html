❓ Help Center

💜 What Number Should I Watch?

For day-to-day decisions, focus on Paycheck To Paycheck. This number shows what should remain after the next paycheck arrives and the bills tied to that paycheck have been paid.

🏠 Dashboard

Dashboard provides a live paycheck-to-paycheck snapshot of finances. Available To Spend shows current cash after bills due before the next paycheck. Safe Spend applies any selected buffer. Paycheck To Paycheck shows what should remain after the next paycheck arrives and bills before the following paycheck are paid.

📌 Coming Up

Displays unpaid bills and unreceived income expected during the next 30 days.

💰 Paychecks

Shows all scheduled paychecks for the year. Marking income as received updates forecasts, balances, and reports.

📈 Forecasts

NOW focuses on current finances and remaining activity for the active budget period. PROJECTED includes all future scheduled income and expenses to estimate long-term financial outcomes.

📊 Monthly Summary

Provides a summary of income received, income remaining, bills paid, and bills still due. Use this page to review overall progress during the current budgeting cycle.

📅 Timeline

Displays upcoming future financial events in chronological order.

📖 Ledger

Permanent historical record of completed income and expense activity.

🎯 Goals

Goals are informational only and never affect forecasting calculations.

✅ Reconcile

Update actual checking and savings balances from your bank accounts. Accurate balances improve forecasts, available spending calculations, and dashboard insights.

📜 Activity Log

Automatically records major events including bills paid, income received, imports, exports, and balance updates.

☁️ Cloud Sync

Budget data is automatically backed up to cloud storage when changes are saved. Use Sync Now to manually load the latest budget from the cloud.

🛡️ Safe Spend

Safe Spend shows how much cash is available after subtracting bills due before the next paycheck and applying your selected buffer. This is the safest number to use when deciding whether extra spending is affordable.

💰 Paycheck To Paycheck

Paycheck To Paycheck estimates how much cash should remain after the next paycheck arrives and the bills between that paycheck and the following paycheck are paid. This is one of the most useful indicators for short-term financial planning.

📈 Forecast Types

CURRENT MONTH shows expected income, expenses, and projected cash position for the remainder of the current month.
PROJECTED shows all remaining scheduled income and expenses through the end of the budget timeline and includes the Year End Projection value.
