Paycheck Pilot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

What we collect

Your email address (for sign-in), an optional display name, and the financial information you choose to enter — accounts and balances, bills, transactions, goals, and your paycheck amount and schedule. If you turn on notifications, we store your device's push-notification subscription so we can send payday and bill reminders. We don't collect anything else about you.

Bank statement imports

When you import a bank statement (CSV), it's analyzed on your device. Only the accounts, bills and transactions you choose to import are saved. Paycheck Pilot never asks for your bank username or password and doesn't connect to your bank.

How it's stored

Your data is kept on your device so the app works offline, and synced over encrypted connections to our cloud database (Supabase) so it's available on your other devices. We don't share your financial information with data brokers, advertisers or sponsors.

Household sharing

If you join or create a household, anything you mark Household is visible to every member of that household, and your paycheck amount and schedule are shared with them so the household forecast includes it. Anything marked Personal stays visible only to you.

Sponsored partner links

Some screens (including Flight Deck) show sponsored banners for partner services, provided through the AWIN affiliate network. When a banner is shown, its image loads from AWIN's servers, which — like any website serving an image — receive standard technical information such as your IP address and device/browser type. If you tap a banner, you're taken to the partner's website through an AWIN tracking link; AWIN and the partner may use cookies, and we may earn a commission. We never send your financial data, email address or any account information to sponsors or AWIN. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave Paycheck Pilot.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share your financial data with advertisers.

Your data, your control

You can export a complete backup of your data at any time from Settings → Data Management. You can delete your account and data in the app from Settings → Account & Privacy → Delete My Account, or request deletion without the app on our account deletion page or by emailing support@paycheckpilot.app from your account's email address.

Children's privacy

Paycheck Pilot is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@paycheckpilot.app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, let you know in the app.
